# Verified sources and claim boundaries

ASSERA uses these sources for problem context and WebMCP release guidance. Each
claim below preserves its population and year.

## KFF — Medicare Advantage, 2024

Source: [Medicare Advantage insurers made nearly 53 million prior authorization determinations in 2024](https://www.kff.org/medicare/medicare-advantage-insurers-made-nearly-53-million-prior-authorization-determinations-in-2024/)

KFF’s analysis of 2024 Medicare Advantage data reports nearly 53 million prior
authorization determinations, 4.1 million fully or partially denied requests,
an 11.5% appeal rate among denied requests, and an 80.7% partial/full overturn
rate among appealed denials. These Medicare Advantage figures must not be
generalized to all U.S. insurance. An overturn does not by itself prove the
initial decision was improper.

## American Medical Association — physician survey

Source: [AMA survey: prior authorization reform pledge falls short for physicians](https://www.ama-assn.org/press-center/ama-press-releases/ama-survey-prior-authorization-reform-pledge-falls-short-physicians)

The AMA reports findings from a survey of 1,000 practicing physicians: 95%
reported delays to necessary care, 79% reported treatment abandonment linked
to authorization challenges, and 26% reported serious adverse events
associated with prior authorization. These are physician-reported survey
findings, not causal estimates for every patient or payer.

## CMS Interoperability and Prior Authorization Final Rule

Source: [CMS-0057-F fact sheet](https://www.cms.gov/newsroom/fact-sheets/cms-interoperability-prior-authorization-final-rule-cms-0057-f)

CMS requires impacted payers to provide a specific reason for denied prior
authorization decisions for covered non-drug items/services. Timing must be
described carefully: CMS says operational provisions generally begin in 2026,
while the API requirements—including denial reasons in Prior Authorization API
responses—generally begin January 1, 2027. ASSERA does not claim that every
specific-denial-reason API obligation begins in 2026.

## Project claim rules

- No claim of real insurer integration, HIPAA compliance, clinical validation,
  medical/legal advice, or appeal-success prediction.
- All case and receipt data shown by ASSERA are synthetic.
- Statistics retain population/year and direct source links.
- Product statements describe demonstrated behavior, not future capability.
